Frequently asked questions

Does BlueCore put my account at risk?

BlueCore runs locally on your device and never touches your account credentials; as with any third-party software, use is at your own responsibility.

Will I get banned on servers?

BlueCore doesn't include modules that break fairness between players — only performance and visual customization. Always check the rules of the server you join.

When do cosmetics unlock?

Cosmetics are a snapshot preview for the upcoming v2 release and can't be equipped yet. They'll unlock once v2 ships.

Is there an iOS version?

Only Android is in development right now. iOS isn't on the roadmap.
